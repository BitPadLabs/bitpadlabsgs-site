We are proud to announce that **BitPadLabs Government Solutions LLC (BPL-GS)** has achieved official **Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB)** certification.

![SBA SDVOSB Logo]({{ "/assets/images/sba-logo-png/sba-sdvosb.png" | relative_url }}){: style="max-width: 320px; margin: 1rem 0;" }

## Veteran-Led Mission

This certification reflects our veteran-led identity and our commitment to serving federal agencies, particularly the Department of Veterans Affairs. As a certified SDVOSB, BPL-GS brings both mission focus and acquisition advantages to the work of modernizing federal technology systems.

## What the Certification Means

The SDVOSB certification enables BPL-GS to pursue and support set-aside opportunities while continuing to deliver the capabilities our team is known for:

- **Enterprise cloud platform governance** with GovCloud and NIST-aligned baselines
- **DevSecOps and CI/CD automation** for secure, repeatable delivery
- **Observability and data governance** for FedRAMP-ready systems
- **GitHub Enterprise modernization** at enterprise scale
- **TBM-aligned cost transparency** for federal portfolios

## Team Expertise in Support of Veterans

Our team brings federal modernization experience from large agencies with deep focus on security, accessibility, and governance. The SDVOSB certification strengthens our ability to partner with agencies, primes, and mission teams serving veterans.

## Looking Ahead

As a newly certified SDVOSB, BPL-GS is ready to support veterans-focused modernization priorities across healthcare IT, benefits systems, compliance, and cloud infrastructure.
